# 🏊 42 Piscine — 42 İstanbul

This repository contains the exercises and projects I completed during the **42 İstanbul Piscine**, an intensive programming training and selection program focused on learning through practice, problem-solving, and peer-based learning.

Throughout the Piscine, I worked primarily with **C programming and Unix Shell**, progressing through a series of exercises designed to strengthen programming fundamentals and algorithmic thinking.

## 🎯 About the Piscine

The 42 Piscine is an intensive learning experience where participants solve programming challenges independently and collaboratively rather than following traditional classroom-based lessons.

During this process, I practiced breaking problems into smaller parts, debugging my own code, understanding errors, and improving solutions through continuous practice.

## 💻 What I Worked On

During the Piscine, I worked through exercises covering different aspects of C programming and command-line environments.

The process involved writing programs from scratch, compiling and testing them, identifying errors, and refining solutions according to the requirements of each exercise.

As the modules progressed, the exercises gradually required more attention to program structure, logic, and problem-solving.

## 🌱 What the Experience Gave Me

The Piscine was one of my first intensive experiences with **C and system-oriented programming environments**.

More importantly, it helped me become more comfortable with approaching unfamiliar programming problems independently. Instead of only focusing on getting the expected output, I learned to spend more time understanding why a solution works, debugging mistakes, and improving my approach when it doesn't.

Working in a peer-learning environment also gave me experience discussing different solutions and learning from other approaches.
